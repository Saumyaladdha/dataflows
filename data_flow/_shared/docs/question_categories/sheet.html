The categories

Derived from the corpus, not assumed: each count is how many of the classified questions fall in that category. The last two are not exam traps — they are defects in our source material, where the printed question has no single answer or its options were never captured. They are separated because the response differs: a trap is solved, a defect is re-acquired.
