# Superseded A2S outputs — transcribed from a crop that cut off content

Each of these was named by the worker that transcribed it as losing content at a crop edge, most
often the caption band sitting just below a figure: "Figure 1.10 A wind-pollinated plant showing
compact inflorecence and well-" then nothing (BIO12L-ch01-p011-b3), or a table title reduced to its
second line "and Genes" with "Table 4.3: ..." above the frame (BIO12L-ch04-p014-b2).

They were cropped with symmetric padding, which the reports showed is the wrong shape: NCERT puts a
TABLE's title ABOVE it and a FIGURE's caption BELOW it, and in-figure labels crowd the top edge.
Padding is now asymmetric by kind -- table 96 top / 56 bottom, figure 72 top / 96 bottom, 56 each
side throughout -- so these are re-cropped and re-transcribed rather than left with a truncated
caption.

Kept, not deleted: real agent work, and the transcriptions of the figures' LABELS in them are
mostly complete; it is the captions that were cut.
