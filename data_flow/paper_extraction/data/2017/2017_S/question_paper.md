## Q1

Biology · zoology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation

The genotypes of a Husband and Wife are $\mathrm{I^AI^B}$ and $\mathrm{I^Ai}$.
Among the blood types of their children, how many different genotypes and phenotypes are possible ?

- (1) 4 genotypes ; 3 phenotypes
- (2) 4 genotypes ; 4 phenotypes
- (3) 3 genotypes ; 3 phenotypes
- (4) 3 genotypes ; 4 phenotypes

**Answer** — booklet: (none) · independent solve: (1) · unchecked

## Q2

Biology · zoology · Body Fluids and Circulation

Adult human RBCs are enucleate. Which of the following statement(s) is/are most appropriate explanation for this feature ?

- **((a))** They do not need to reproduce
- **((b))** They are somatic cells
- **((c))** They do not metabolize
- **((d))** All their internal space is available for oxygen transport

- (1) (a), (c) and (d)
- (2) (b) and (c)
- (3) Only (d)
- (4) Only (a)

**Answer** — booklet: (none) · independent solve: (3) · unchecked

**Solved independently** (answer withheld)

4. Statement (d) holds: without a nucleus more internal space is available for haemoglobin and hence $O_2$ transport, so the answer is Only (d), option $3$.

## Q12

Asymptote in a logistic growth curve is obtained when :

- (1) $K > N$

- (4) $K = N$

**Answer** — booklet: (none) · independent solve: (4) · unchecked

## Q91

A first order reaction has a specific reaction rate of $10^{-2}\,\mathrm{sec^{-1}}$. How much time will it take for $20\,\mathrm{g}$ of the reactant to reduce to $5\,\mathrm{g}$ ?

- (2) $693.0\,\mathrm{sec}$
- (3) $238.6\,\mathrm{sec}$
- (4) $138.6\,\mathrm{sec}$

**Answer** — booklet: (none) · independent solve: (4) · unchecked

**Solved independently** (answer withheld)

1. For a first order reaction, $t = \frac{2.303}{k}\log\frac{[A]_0}{[A]}$.

3. $t = \frac{2.303}{10^{-2}} \times 0.602 = 230.3 \times 0.602 = 138.6\,\mathrm{sec}$.

## Q179

Physics · Thermodynamics

A gas mixture consists of 2 moles of $\mathrm{O_2}$ and 4 moles of $\mathrm{Ar}$ at temperature $T$. Neglecting all vibrational modes, the total internal energy of the system is :

- (1) $9\,RT$
- (2) $11\,RT$
- (3) $4\,RT$
- (4) $15\,RT$

**Answer** — booklet: (none) · independent solve: (2) · unchecked

**Solved independently** (answer withheld)

1. Without vibrational modes, $\mathrm{O_2}$ (diatomic) has $f = 5$ degrees of freedom and $\mathrm{Ar}$ (monatomic) has $f = 3$.
2. Internal energy per mole is $\frac{f}{2}RT$, so $U_{\mathrm{O_2}} = 2 \times \frac{5}{2}RT = 5RT$.
3. $U_{\mathrm{Ar}} = 4 \times \frac{3}{2}RT = 6RT$.
4. $U = 5RT + 6RT = 11RT$, which is option $2$.

## Q180

Physics · Current Electricity

The resistance of a wire is 'R' ohm. If it is melted and stretched to 'n' times its original length, its new resistance will be :

- (1) $n^{2}R$
- (2) $\dfrac{R}{n^{2}}$
- (3) $nR$
- (4) $\dfrac{R}{n}$

**Answer** — booklet: (none) · independent solve: (1) · unchecked

**Solved independently** (answer withheld)

1. Melting and recasting keeps the volume constant: $lA = l'A'$.
2. With $l' = nl$, the new area is $A' = \frac{A}{n}$.
3. $R' = \frac{\rho (nl)}{A/n} = n^{2}\frac{\rho l}{A}$.
4. $R' = n^{2}R$, which is option $1$.
